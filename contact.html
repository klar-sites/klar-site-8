Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

How quickly do you respond to messages?

We typically respond within 24 hours during business days. For urgent matters, you can reach us directly at hello@inkwell.blog and we'll do our best to get back to you sooner.

Do you accept guest posts?

Yes! We welcome well-written, original articles on web development, design, and productivity. Please reach out via the contact form with your topic idea and a brief writing sample.

Can I republish your articles?

All articles are published under a Creative Commons license. You're welcome to share and reference our content with proper attribution and a link back to the original post.

Are you available for consulting or collaboration?

We're selectively open to design system consulting, technical writing partnerships, and speaking engagements. Send us a message with details about your project and we'll let you know if it's a fit.
